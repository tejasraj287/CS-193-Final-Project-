# Food Inventory Tracker

This program keeps track of the food in your fridge and its expiration dates to reduce food waste and money lost.

## The Problem

Everyone has a fridge at home, but people often forget what food they have and when its going to expire. This results in food wasted and money lost. Keeping track of your groceries can be tedious since different items have many different expiration dates. 

## Tech Stack

- **Language:** Java
- **Libraries:** any external dependencies
- **External tools:** IO.readln(), ArrayList, FileReader and FileWriter

## MVP

- Add/Edit/Delete food items in inventory
- Store expiration dates
- Display items in the fridge
- Show which foods are expiring soon or are expired
